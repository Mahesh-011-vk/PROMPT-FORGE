# Image Prompting Guide: Midjourney, FLUX, and Stable Diffusion

## 1. Composition and Camera Perspective
- Use physical focal lengths: 24mm (wide environmental), 35mm (documentary/film still), 85mm (portrait bokeh).
- Declare camera hardware: Hasselblad H6D-100c for high dynamic range studio photography; ARRI Alexa 65 for cinematic motion picture frame captures.

## 2. Lighting and Volumetric Design
- Avoid generic terms like "good lighting".
- Specify lighting dynamics: golden hour sidelight, moody neon rim light, soft diffuse studio box, volumetric god rays through mist.

## 3. Materials and Textures
- To avoid plastic AI surfaces, explicitly mention micro-textures: porous skin, brushed anodized aluminum, woven linen, weathered stone with moss.
